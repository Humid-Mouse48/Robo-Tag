ROBO TAG

A fully virtual multiplayer tag world. Push off the ground and platforms with your hands to move, then tag the player glowing red.

VR: gravity lowers you until your tracked hands physically contact the floor; hands cannot pass through it. Plant a hand and swing/push against the floor or platforms. Only moving arm strokes against the floor or platforms propel you; holding a hand still will not move you. Momentum slows when you stop swinging. Turn with the right thumbstick. Press Y on the left controller to show its wrist-mounted settings menu. Desktop: WASD to move, Space to jump, mouse to look.
